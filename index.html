About Us

VSA is the premier outlet to celebrate Vietnamese Culture on Wash U’s campus. We are a Cultural and Diversity Group with a focus on advocacy, inclusion, and leadership. Our mission is to unite students through Vietnamese Culture and to build a community of International students, Vietnamese-American students, and anyone with an interest in learning more about Vietnamese culture. Events and meetings shall strive to meet this purpose.
